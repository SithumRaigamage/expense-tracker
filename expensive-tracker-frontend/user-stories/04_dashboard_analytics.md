# User Story: Dashboard & Advanced Analytics

## Current Status
- High-level metrics (Total Income, Total Expenses, Net Savings).
- Monthly stat charts.
- Recent activity feed.

## Proposed Improvements for Individual Use

### 1. Customizable "Widget" Dashboard [IMPLEMENTED]
- **Story**: As a user, I want to pin specific widgets (e.g., "Food Spending vs Budget", "Net Worth over 6 months") to my dashboard so I see what matters most to me first.
- **Status**: ✅ Implemented with persistent local storage.
- **Benefit**: Personalized financial overview.

### 2. Spending Trend Analysis [IMPLEMENTED]
- **Story**: As a user, I want to see a line chart comparing my spending across different months to identify seasonal patterns.
- **Status**: ✅ Implemented with multi-year comparison series.
- **Benefit**: Helps in long-term financial planning and identifying anomalies.

### 3. Expense Breakdown (Sankey or Sunburst Diagrams)
- **Story**: As a user, I want a visual flow of how my income is distributed into categories and sub-categories.
- **Benefit**: Intuitive understanding of "where the money goes" at a glance.

### 4. Predictive Insights
- **Story**: As a user, I want the app to predict my end-of-month balance based on my current spending habits and upcoming recurring bills.
- **Benefit**: Early warning system for potential overspending.
