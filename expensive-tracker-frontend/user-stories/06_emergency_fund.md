# User Story: Emergency Fund & Savings Goals

## Current Status
- Progress tracking for a single emergency fund.
- Basic status visualization.

## Proposed Improvements for Individual Use

### 1. Smart Emergency Fund Calculator
- **Story**: As a user, I want the app to suggest an emergency fund target based on 3-6 months of my average actual expenses.
- **Benefit**: Realistic financial goal setting based on data, not guesses.

### 2. Multi-Goal Support
- **Story**: As a user, I want to track multiple savings goals simultaneously (e.g., "New Laptop", "Down Payment", "Vacation") with separate progress bars.
- **Benefit**: Motivates saving for specific life events.

### 3. Milestone Celebrations
- **Story**: As a user, I want to receive a notification or visual badge when I reach 25%, 50%, or 100% of a goal.
- **Benefit**: Gamification to encourage better financial habits.

### 4. Contribution History
- **Story**: As a user, I want to see a timeline of how much and when I contributed to a specific goal.
- **Benefit**: Tracks saving consistency over time.
