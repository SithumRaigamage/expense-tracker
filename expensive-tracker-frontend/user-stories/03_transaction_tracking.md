# User Story: Transaction Tracking

## Current Status
- Users can record income and expenses.
- Categorization of transactions.
- Basic bulk add functionality.
- View recent transactions on the dashboard.

## Proposed Improvements for Individual Use

### 1. Recurring Transactions (Automated Tracking)
- **Story**: As a user, I want to set up recurring transactions (e.g., monthly rent, salary, Netflix subscription) so that the system records them automatically on the specified date.
- **Benefit**: Saves time and ensures regular bills/income are never forgotten.

### 2. Smart Categorization (AI-Powered)
- **Story**: As a user, I want the app to suggest or automatically assign categories based on my previous transaction descriptions (e.g., "Uber" -> "Transportation").
- **Benefit**: Reduces manual effort and improves consistency in data.

### 3. Receipt Scanning & OCR
- **Story**: As a user, I want to take a photo of a physical receipt and have the app extract the amount, date, and merchant automatically.
- **Benefit**: Makes it effortless to track cash expenses on the go.

### 4. Advanced Search & Filtering
- **Story**: As a user, I want to search transactions by keyword, amount range, or specific date clusters to find historical data quickly.
- **Benefit**: Better data retrieval for personal audits or tax purposes.
