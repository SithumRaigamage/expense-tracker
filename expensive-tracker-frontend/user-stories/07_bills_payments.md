# User Story: Bills & Subscription Management

## Current Status
- List of upcoming bills.
- Basic tracking of recurring payments.

## Proposed Improvements for Individual Use

### 1. Calendar View for Bills
- **Story**: As a user, I want to see my upcoming bills on a monthly calendar view to visualize "heavy payment days".
- **Benefit**: Better cash flow planning throughout the month.

### 2. "Mark as Paid" Integration
- **Story**: As a user, when I mark a bill as paid, I want the app to automatically create a transaction in the correct wallet.
- **Benefit**: Reduces double-entry and ensures records are up to date.

### 3. Subscription Auditor
- **Story**: As a user, I want a dedicated view of all my active subscriptions (Netflix, Spotify, Gym) and their annual total cost.
- **Benefit**: Identify "subscription creep" and unnecessary expenses.

### 4. Overdue Notifications
- **Story**: As a user, I want an urgent alert if a bill is past its due date and haven't been marked as paid.
- **Benefit**: Prevents late fees and credit score impacts.
